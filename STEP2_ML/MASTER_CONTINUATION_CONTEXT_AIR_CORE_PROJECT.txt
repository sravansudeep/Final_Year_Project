IMPORTANT:
    The correct drainage-time column name everywhere is:

        td_to

    NOT:

        td/to

Use "td_to" in all future scripts, Excel sheets, DataFrames, model code,
plots, formulas, filenames, and ML work.

TARGET DEFINITIONS:

1) hc_hi
    Dimensionless / normalized critical air-core height:
        hc / hi

2) td_to
    Dimensionless / normalized drainage time:
        td / to

3) Vch
    Characteristic-volume response.

IMPORTANT:
    Vch should NOT be treated as a literal arithmetic product of the Excel
    response columns A and hc.

The source-paper formulation describes characteristic volume as a normalized
quantity combining air-core height and blockage:
    Vch = A_air * hc / (A_total * hi)

The source paper also describes hc/hi and td/to as normalized quantities and
argues that critical height alone does not fully quantify vortexing because
blockage matters.

Source files present in the project/library include:
    JSR2023.pdf
    JSR2023(1).pdf
    JSR2023(2).pdf

Source paper:
    Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets, 2023-era paper
    “New Parameter to Characterize Rankine Vortex Formation in Liquid
     Draining Tanks”
    DOI: 10.2514/1.A35553

Do not re-derive or alter the source definitions unless the source is explicitly
reviewed.
